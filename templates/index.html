How it works

One platform for capture, cleaning, and reporting — so managers act on reliable numbers, not scattered spreadsheets.

Capture

Staff upload Excel/CSV or enter records manually. The system flags contamination and shows a review screen before anything is saved.

Clean & download

Missing values, negatives, and word-numbers are corrected. Staff keep a clean dataset; organization admins work from trusted data.

Report & decide

Org admins set targets, generate performance reports, and print or save PDF views for revenue, patients, compliance, and more.

Built for polyclinic roles

Each organization stays isolated — your staff, your KPIs, your reports.

Staff

Platform admin
