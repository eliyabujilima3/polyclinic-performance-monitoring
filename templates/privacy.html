Privacy & ethics

What this platform stores

This system supports organizational performance monitoring. Uploads may include operational data and, when present, personal columns.

Anonymization

  • Columns that look like personal identifiers (names, phones, emails, national IDs, MRN, addresses, etc.) are anonymized for on-screen review and stored datasets.
  • Values are replaced with stable tokens such as ANON_Name_0001.
  • When you download Excel or CSV, original values are restored from a secure mapping kept with that dataset.

Accountability

Key actions (upload, save, download, report generate/print, suspend) are written to an audit log with user and timestamp.

Retention

Unfinished review drafts in uploads/ are removed after 48 hours. Organizations should only keep cleaned datasets needed for reporting.
