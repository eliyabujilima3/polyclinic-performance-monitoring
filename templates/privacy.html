Privacy & ethics

What this platform stores

This system is for organizational performance monitoring using aggregate operational data (department revenue, patient volumes, diagnoses counts, feedback categories, etc.).

What must not be uploaded

  • Patient names, phone numbers, emails, national IDs / NIDA, passport numbers
  • Medical record numbers (MRN), home addresses, next-of-kin details
  • Photos, fingerprints, or other direct identifiers

Uploads containing those column names are blocked automatically. Staff must also tick a privacy confirmation before upload.

Accountability

Key actions (upload, save, download, report generate/print, suspend) are written to an audit log with user and timestamp — not the clinical row contents.

Retention

Unfinished review drafts in uploads/ are removed after 48 hours. Organizations should only keep cleaned aggregate datasets needed for reporting.
